# H11 idea audit — counterpart-relation specificity and architecture replication

**Status:** read-only ideation record, 2026-08-11. This document does not
modify H9, H10, the paper, data, or any result. It opens no new dataset row,
audio payload, label, score, prediction, or metric. It is a proposal for a
fresh protocol, not permission to run one.

## Starting evidence and non-negotiable boundaries

H9 is a well-controlled positive study: same-item ranking (P) beat both
same-pool BCE (B1) and an equal-edge random-pair ranking control (B2) on two
fixed targets using fresh Res2TCNGuard. H10 is useful directional evidence on
a third target, but its 8.30% reduction against B1 misses its prospectively
fixed 10% practical-effect rule. H10 is terminal; it may not be relaxed,
re-analysed, or used to choose a new target.

The principal remaining scientific uncertainty is not whether *a margin loss*
can help. H9's B2 handles that. It is whether the gain is specific to the
documented **same-item relation**, rather than a relation it bundles (notably
source voice, recording chain, duration, or other metadata). H9 itself
explicitly does not establish a semantic or causal internal mechanism.

The next study must therefore satisfy all of the following.

- Leave H9 and H10 source ledgers, checkpoints, targets, metrics, and claims
  untouched. H10's CD-ADD target is historically exposed and unavailable to
  a new terminal firewall.
- Do not use any H8/H9/H10 target for a new terminal claim. In particular,
  CFAD, CVoiceFake-small, DECRO, LibriSeVoc, XMAD, SONAR, ArAD, and CD-ADD are
  excluded. The earlier five-corpus audit targets and DeepVoice are excluded
  too.
- Start with a committed H11-only source/edge protocol, synthetic-only
  implementation tests, and an independent metadata-only target-lineage audit.
  Target audio and labels remain inaccessible until the full source checkpoint
  ledger is sealed.
- Train only fresh models in BF16. Pin code revisions; keep all downloads,
  caches, checkpoints, and raw predictions on the HDD; commit only compact
  ledgers, code, hashes, and notes.
- Report all frozen methods, architectures, seeds, and targets. No target
  swap, subset, loss change, threshold relaxation, or best-seed selection is
  allowed after target access.

These boundaries turn the H10 non-pass into a useful design constraint instead
of a reason to search for a friendlier benchmark.

## Structured divergence

The candidate set was generated with three complementary lenses: (i) the
**tension** between pairwise supervision and attribution, (ii) **failure
analysis** of H9's single-backbone mechanism ambiguity and H10's practical
effect miss, and (iii) **decomposition** of a pair into item, voice, generator,
and random components. Scores are qualitative (5 is best) and rank the next
*study*, not expected EER.

| Rank | Candidate | Core falsifiable question | Impact | Attribution | Feasibility | Novelty safety | Decision |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | **Counterpart Specificity Ladder (CSL)** | Does an exact documented counterpart edge beat both a voice-matched/different-item edge and a stratified random edge across new architectures and targets? | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | **Advance.** |
| 2 | Straight AASIST H9 replication | Does H9's B1/B2/P result repeat with fresh AASIST on new targets? | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | Keep as the CSL's minimal backbone check, but alone it leaves the mechanism ambiguity. |
| 3 | Two-backbone H9 replication only | Does P beat B1/B2 for AASIST and RawNet2 on a fresh panel? | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Useful replication, but add CSL's voice control so a positive result says more than “another architecture worked.” |
| 4 | Same-item supervised contrastive or triplet replacement | Does a new contrastive loss beat the H9 hinge? | 3 | 2 | 3 | 1 | Reject: pairwise/contrastive anti-spoofing already exists and this becomes post-result loss search. |
| 5 | Pair-mined/adaptive edge weights | Can learned hard-pair mining improve transfer? | 3 | 1 | 2 | 1 | Reject: mining would make source composition and optimisation confounds harder, with weak causal attribution. |
| 6 | Target-unlabelled adaptation or calibration | Can target statistics improve a PCR detector? | 3 | 1 | 3 | 2 | Reject for the primary paper: it changes the deployment assumption and risks target-specific selection. |
| 7 | Codec/quality augmentation plus PCR | Does PCR synergise with augmentation? | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | Park: H2/H2B did not establish a quality-preserving transform family; combining it now would reopen a closed issue. |
| 8 | Source-generator leave-one-out PCR | Does same-item ranking generalise to a held-out ODSS generator? | 3 | 3 | 5 | 2 | Supplementary diagnostic only: ODSS has too few documented generation families for a convincing primary generalisation claim. |
| 9 | New paired source dataset (for example, CVoiceFake) | Does PCR transfer when trained on a second paired source? | 5 | 4 | 1 | 4 | Long-term priority, but not the next two-week study: counterpart verification and fresh source tooling are substantial. |
| 10 | Revisit crest-factor conditioning | Can a signal feature condition PCR? | 2 | 1 | 3 | 1 | Reject: H1 portability and H2/H2B quality gates are terminal and cannot choose a new mitigation. |

The deliberate simplicity test eliminates candidates 4--7: a successful
method should first show *which documented relation* matters before adding a
new loss, adaptive sampler, augmentation, or target adaptation. The
stakeholder test also favours CSL: an operator gets a compact, fresh-trained
detector; a reviewer gets a direct relation-level falsification; and a
dataset curator gets a concrete reason to preserve counterpart and speaker
provenance.

## Winner: Counterpart Specificity Ladder (CSL)

**Two-sentence pitch.** Speech deepfake detectors often train on labels alone
or on generic pairwise objectives, but H9 cannot distinguish a documented
natural--synthetic counterpart edge from the voice and recording context it
shares. CSL keeps the data, hinge count, optimiser envelope, and random-edge
control fixed while inserting a same-voice/different-item control, then
replicates the resulting relation ordering with fresh graph-attention and
raw-waveform backbones on a predeclared, unseen external panel.

This is problem-first rather than “a new loss”: the problem is an
under-identified positive result. Its expected contribution is a conditional
empirical statement about **relation specificity**, not a claim that the
network learned semantics, language, or a causal content representation.

### Core H11 question and hypothesis

> With the same source items, edge count, margin loss, and training recipe,
> does a documented same-item natural--synthetic edge reduce external EER more
> than (a) BCE, (b) a stratum-matched random opposite-class edge, and (c) a
> same-source-voice but different-item edge?

Let `C` denote counterpart edges, `V` voice-matched/different-item edges, and
`R` random stratified edges. The primary directional hypothesis is
`EER(C) < EER(V), EER(R), EER(BCE)` for each frozen architecture and target.
It is falsifiable in an especially informative way: if `V` matches `C`, H9's
benefit is not specific to item identity; if `R` matches `C`, it is not
specific to pairing; and if none helps, the H9 result is limited rather than
retuned away.

### Proposed source relation ladder

This is a new H11 source freeze, not an edit to the H9 freeze. It may use the
public ODSS source only after a new, metadata-only builder verifies the
following hard contract.

| ID | Training objective | Edge relation | What it isolates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B | BCE | none | Ordinary label supervision on the identical relation-complete pool. |
| R | BCE + hinge | H9-style label-valid random bona-fide partner, matched on frozen language/source/generator strata | Margin regularisation and coarse source composition. |
| V | BCE + hinge | Same documented `voice_key`, **different** documented `item_key`; no counterpart identity | Voice/recording-family correspondence without an item counterpart. |
| C | BCE + hinge | Same documented `item_key`, natural--synthetic counterpart | The full documented counterpart relation. |

For every spoof endpoint retained in the H11 source pool, the builder must
find one valid `C`, `V`, and `R` partner *before* training. The four methods
then consume the same relation-complete trial pool; R/V/C use exactly the same
number of edges and identical spoof endpoints. The builder must prove no V
edge shares `item_key` with its spoof endpoint, retain exact marginal tables
for language/source/generator/voice/duration, and deterministically resolve
ties. If it cannot make those endpoint and marginal contracts hold, CSL stops
rather than silently changing a control.

The existing H9 metadata does not prove transcript equality or a semantic
mechanism. Accordingly, `C` is called *same-item*, never “semantic,” and the
only intended inference from `C > V` is that this documented item relation is
more useful than the stated voice-matched alternative in the frozen setting.

### Architecture and target replication plan

The recommended full study uses two independently trainable implementations
from the official AASIST codebase, both fresh-initialised:

1. **AASIST**: integrated spectro-temporal graph attention; and
2. **RawNet2**: a raw-waveform baseline implemented by the same official
   training repository.

This avoids treating the Arena's inference-only ONNX/checkpoint artifacts as
trainable models and provides two material architecture changes relative to
H9's fresh 172k-parameter Res2TCNGuard. The official AASIST repository
documents both training paths; all supplied/pretrained weights remain
prohibited. A synthetic-only BF16 qualification selects the fastest safe
batch/worker pair per backbone before ODSS audio is read.

The candidate **fresh terminal panel** is deliberately specified now from
public card metadata only:

- `SpeechAntiSpoofingBenchmarks/HABLA` at
  `764c00726cc4327ff2a270b8347375e95a7034db` (Spanish/Latin-American accent
  speech, CC-BY-4.0); and
- `SpeechAntiSpoofingBenchmarks/EmoFake_test` at
  `a49bd1c2110e199421055fd2f2561c8eb9e43c13` (emotional speech/deepfake
  detection, CC-BY-4.0).

These identifiers were discovered from public Hub repository metadata on
2026-08-11, not from any row, waveform, label value, score, prediction, or
metric. They are not H8/H9/H10 terminal targets. Before an H11 protocol is
committed, a separate metadata-only audit must pin card and tree hashes,
binary-audio schema, licence, size, and source-lineage risks. It must hard-stop
a target with known ODSS recording-source/speaker/released-item overlap;
zero waveform collision alone does not establish speaker or text independence.
No replacement target is permitted after this predeclared audit/protocol pair.

Each architecture gets four new seeds, a source-only `C` lambda selection grid
with fixed tie rule shared by R/V/C, a finite maximum epoch budget, and a
source-development checkpoint rule. All final checkpoints must be sealed
before either target is materialised. The two-pass target firewall is: (1)
`path,audio` only to opaque IDs, copied bytes, and canonical 16-kHz
fingerprints; (2) `path,label` only to a separate label table. A terminal
evaluator writes the complete architecture/method/seed prediction matrix
before its one label join.

### Baselines, ablations, and terminal rule

The main comparison has four methods, two fresh architectures, two targets,
and four seeds per cell. It therefore answers a relation question rather than
a single aggregate leaderboard question.

- **Primary endpoint:** four-seed probability ensemble EER, unweighted over
  the two H11 targets within each architecture; EER and AUROC are reported for
  every architecture × target × method cell.
- **Primary contrasts:** `C-B`, `C-R`, and `C-V`, with 2,000 shared sample-ID,
  label-stratified bootstrap draws per architecture (and a predeclared,
  architecture-stratified combined summary). Seed-level values are mandatory;
  uncertainty is conditional on each frozen four-seed ensemble.
- **Confirmatory pass:** C must be at least 10% relatively lower in each
  architecture-level two-target macro EER than **all three** comparators; have
  lower point EER in all four architecture × target cells; have all six
  architecture-level paired bootstrap intervals below zero; and pass source
  reconstruction, target-lineage, collision, and prediction-order audits.

This is intentionally demanding. If C only beats B/R but not V, the accurate
conclusion is that a pairwise advantage was replicated but **counterpart
specificity was not**. If one architecture or target fails, report the full
matrix and stop; do not promote a subset, tune relation rules, or make a
multi-architecture claim.

## Novelty boundary and anticipated reviewer objection

Pairwise and contrastive anti-spoofing are not new: Xie et al. use random
same-/different-class Siamese pairs, Tran et al. use class-supervised
contrastive learning, and Kim et al. align augmentations of a bona-fide
utterance. The novelty is neither the hinge loss nor a claim to be the first
pair-aware detector. It is a matched *relation decomposition*: exact
documented counterpart versus same-voice/different-item versus equal-edge
stratified random pair, tested under a source-only fence with fresh
architectures and a new external panel.

The strongest objection is that `C > V` still does not prove that a model
represents linguistic content: an item relation can carry duration, alignment,
recording, and generator covariates. The protocol answers by stating the
narrow claim explicitly, publishing edge-marginal diagnostics, and refusing
semantic/causal language. A future transcript-verified factorial source would
be required for that stronger mechanism claim.

## Strict no-go rules

H11 must stop before any terminal metric if any one of these holds.

1. The H11 source builder cannot construct the exact relation-complete
   C/V/R endpoint set, prove V's different-item condition, or satisfy its
   frozen marginals.
2. Either official implementation cannot pass finite-loss/orientation and
   synthetic BF16 qualification, or a predeclared architecture cannot produce
   a complete source checkpoint ledger. Do not quietly drop the failing
   architecture and claim two-backbone evidence.
3. Either candidate target fails its metadata/lineage/licence/schema audit,
   has source-overlap that cannot be excluded, or fails the canonical waveform
   collision audit. Do not substitute another target after this decision.
4. Target labels, scores, or predictions enter model/relation/architecture/
   batch/worker/lambda/epoch/checkpoint/seed selection, or prediction order is
   not demonstrably label-free.
5. The terminal rule fails. Preserve the all-cell result, report the failed
   condition, and do not add a post-hoc relation, target subset, or loss.

## Bounded two-week pilot

The pilot is designed to create evidence about **feasibility and attribution**
without a target result guiding the method.

| Window | Frozen output | Permitted inputs | Go/no-go signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1--2 | H11 protocol draft, target metadata/lineage audit, and edge-builder specification/tests | Existing H9 source *metadata* and public target cards only | Stop if C/V/R cannot be defined with exact contracts or a target has an unresolvable lineage conflict. |
| Days 3--4 | Pinned official AASIST/RawNet2 source, synthetic adapter/orientation/BF16 tests, measured batch/worker ledgers | Synthetic 64,600-sample waveforms only | Stop if either backbone lacks a stable finite BF16 step or no safe reproducible operating point. |
| Days 5--6 | Metadata-only H11 relation-complete source freeze and hashes | ODSS manifest metadata only | Stop if equal endpoint/marginal diagnostics fail; do not inspect target data. |
| Days 7--10 | Source-only C lambda selection and complete source training ledgers | Frozen H11 ODSS audio plus source dev labels | Stop if any scheduled seed/checkpoint/reconstruction check fails; source dev chooses only the predeclared lambda/checkpoint. |
| Days 11--12 | Generic target materializer/evaluator tests and sealed source ledgers | Synthetic Parquet fixtures plus source artifacts | Stop if two-pass firewall, collision handling, or raw-prediction reconstruction is not testable. |
| Days 13--14 | One terminal, all-cell H11 evaluation and independent audit | Only after all prior freezes: the two pinned targets | Either record a full pass/fail matrix or stop without a rerun. A paper edit requires a new claim review. |

The scheduled workload is 56 source fits at most (two architectures ×
12 C-lambda selection fits plus two architectures × 16 B/R/V/C final fits),
run independently across four GPUs in BF16. It is deliberately a single
predeclared study rather than an open-ended architecture/target search.

## Decision

**Recommend H11 CSL, conditional on the source-relation and target-lineage
gates above.** It is the smallest next study that can make the existing H9
story stronger in two ways at once: it challenges the important untested
alternative explanation (voice-matched rather than same-item supervision) and
tests the relationship with two genuinely different, fresh-trained
architectures on a fixed external panel. Until its prospective protocol,
implementation tests, and complete terminal matrix exist, the current paper
remains the narrow H9 result and must not be amended to imply CSL evidence.
