# Problem

## Research question

Can documented natural--synthetic same-item supervision improve cross-corpus
speech deepfake detection beyond label-only training and an equal-budget generic
pairwise control, under a source-only selection and terminal target firewall?

## Boundary

The question concerns a controlled training comparison, not SOTA performance,
semantic understanding, or a causal account of an internal representation.
All target choices, checkpoint selection, and effect gates must be fixed before
terminal target labels or metrics are accessed.
