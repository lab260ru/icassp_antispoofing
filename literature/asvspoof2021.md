# ASVspoof 2021 challenge context

## Verified source

Yamagishi et al., *ASVspoof 2021: accelerating progress in spoofed and
deepfake speech detection*, ASVspoof 2021, pp. 47--54,
DOI: `10.21437/ASVSPOOF.2021-8`.

- Official record: <https://www.isca-archive.org/asvspoof_2021/yamagishi21_asvspoof.html>
- Verified 2026-08-09 from the official ISCA Archive metadata and abstract.

## What the source establishes

- ASVspoof 2021 retains logical- and physical-access tasks and adds a deepfake
  speech task.
- Its databases and evaluation introduce channel/compression variation; the
  official paper also emphasizes that participants were not given matched
  training/development data for the evaluation condition.

## Paper implication

This supports treating ASVspoof2019_LA and ASVspoof2021_LA/DF as distinct
corpora rather than assuming a per-score correlation transfers across editions
or conditions. It does not support a claim about any specific feature.
