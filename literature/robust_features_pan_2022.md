# Environmental cues versus speech spectral discrimination

## Verified source

Pan et al., *Speaker recognition-assisted robust audio deepfake detection*,
Interspeech 2022, pp. 4202--4206, DOI: `10.21437/Interspeech.2022-72`.

- Official record: <https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2022/pan22_interspeech.html>
- Verified 2026-08-09 from the official ISCA Archive metadata and abstract.

## What the source establishes

- The authors state that environmental cues such as background/device noise can
  be used as classification features but are easily attacked.
- They propose speaker-recognition-assisted representations and report improved
  robustness to noise on ASVspoof2019_LA.

## Paper implication

The source supports the practical motivation for exposing signal cues and
testing transformations, rather than interpreting a strong scalar association
as desired detector behavior. It does not establish causal dependence for the
features audited here.
