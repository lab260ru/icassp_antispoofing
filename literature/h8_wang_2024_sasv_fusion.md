# Wang et al. (2024) — scoring fusion and calibration

- **Source:** [DOI 10.21437/Interspeech.2024-422](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2024-422), [arXiv:2406.10836](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10836)
- **Relevance:** Studies calibration/fusion in spoofing-aware speaker
  verification and shows calibration choices materially change fusion.
- **H8 implication:** a calibrated/rank-normalized fixed fusion baseline is
  required before claiming an effect from corpus-robust training.
