- **Source:** [arXiv:2406.03512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03512)
- **Relevance:** Argues cross-dataset degradation in audio-deepfake detection
  is primarily qualitative distribution difference, not merely added hardness.
- **H8 implication:** optimize and report corpus-macro plus worst-target EER;
  pooled accuracy is unsuitable for the H8 target panel.
