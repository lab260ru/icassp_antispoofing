# Serrano et al. (2025) — OOD SSL-CM fusion

- **Source:** [arXiv:2509.12003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12003)
- **Relevance:** Closest known OOD audio-deepfake fusion result. It reports that
  fusion behavior depends on target corpus: logistic regression can improve one
  target while calibrated-score summation is better on another.
- **H8 implication:** ordinary logistic fusion is a mandatory baseline, not a
  contribution. H8 needs a fixed cross-corpus robust objective and several
  untouched targets.
