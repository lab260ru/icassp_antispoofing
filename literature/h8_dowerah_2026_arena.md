# Dowerah et al. (2026) — Speech Deepfake Arena

- **Source:** [DOI 10.1109/OJSP.2026.3652496](https://doi.org/10.1109/OJSP.2026.3652496)
- **Relevance:** Provides a reproducible multi-model, multi-dataset benchmark
  setting for published speech-deepfake score artifacts.
- **H8 implication:** H8 evaluates frozen published systems through a new
  fusion method; it does not represent the result as retraining or independently
  reproducing every base detector.
