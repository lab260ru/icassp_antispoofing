# Pascu et al. (2024) — generalizable speech deepfake detection

- **Source:** [ISCA Archive](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/pascu24_interspeech.html), [DOI 10.21437/Interspeech.2024-1302](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2024-1302)
- **Relevance:** Demonstrates that frozen SSL representations plus simple
  classifiers/calibration can be a credible multi-dataset generalization route.
- **H8 implication:** a frozen-SSL GroupDRO linear probe is a literature-backed
  fallback if the faster frozen-system fusion gate fails.
