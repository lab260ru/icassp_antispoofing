# Sagawa et al. (2020) — GroupDRO

- **Source:** [arXiv:1911.08731](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731)
- **Relevance:** Establishes adversarial group-loss minimization for group
  shifts and cautions that regularization/early stopping are material.
- **H8 implication:** use fixed source corpus × class groups, matched
  regularization, and source-only inner corpus leave-one-out selection. It is a
  robustness mechanism, not the claimed novelty by itself.
