# Zhang et al. (2022) — score fusion failure mode

- **Source:** [DOI 10.1145/3552466.3556528](https://doi.org/10.1145/3552466.3556528), [arXiv:2210.06818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06818)
- **Relevance:** Documents that deepfake score fusion can be unreliable under
  OOD score-scale/weight mismatch.
- **H8 implication:** motivates monotone rank-space mapping and source-domain
  robust optimization rather than raw-score or target-tuned weights.
