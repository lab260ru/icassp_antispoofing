# Meta-review — main evidence-boundary figure

## Consensus

All three Codex-only reviewers agree that replacing the pass-count atlas with a
sealed two-panel H1/H2 evidence-boundary figure improves the paper's hierarchy
without adding a causal, model-ranking, or supplementary-atlas claim. The eight
allowed input hashes, displayed values, and no-score boundary were verified.

The initial v1 presentation needed clarification, not a new experiment:
reviewers flagged potential confusion between Panel A's five-corpus H1 scope
and Panel B's one-corpus detector-free H2 screen, the absence of an explicit
discovery/held-out divider, and the overly affirmative polarity colour.

## Resolution

The separate layout-only v2 protocol was committed before rendering. It retains
the exact eight inputs, values, scales, orders, and claim boundary, while:

1. limiting the figure-level title to an evidence boundary;
2. labelling H1 as a five-corpus fixed association and visibly dividing locked
   discovery from held-out confirmation rows;
3. labelling H2 as the ASVspoof2019 LA, 1,000-clip detector-free screen and
   stating that no score-eligible retained-pair manifest was frozen;
4. using a neutral polarity-control bar; and
5. tightening the paper caption to state the fixed-analysis wording, the
   fixed model-level portability condition, the individually passing control,
   and the absence of cross-panel inference.

The v2 visual verifier reports **Pass**. The compiled working draft remains
five US-letter pages, with Figure 1 on technical page 4 and references on page
5. The result is accepted as an evidence-conservative internal draft.
