# Codex clarity and presentation review

Scope: read-only ICASSP-style review of the current source/PDF.

The reviewer judged the narrow story coherent: a five-corpus association audit,
a failed portability test for the frozen Spectra-AASIST/full-waveform/spoof
crest candidate, a deliberately non-promoted descriptive atlas, and a
quality-only intervention screen that stops before detector scoring.

The main presentation concern was float order: the atlas previously split the
reference list. The source now flushes technical floats before the bibliography,
producing four technical pages followed by one references-only page. The table
caption and Results opening now identify both completed evidence and the H2
quality-gate outcome. Internal project-diary wording was replaced with a scope
statement, the unrun H3 item is framed as a conditional prespecification, and
the abstract now names the frozen crest-factor candidate precisely.

Remaining optional improvements for a future revision are a cleaner atlas label
presentation or legend and a concrete public archival locator once disclosure
policy permits. They are not represented as completed assets in this draft.
