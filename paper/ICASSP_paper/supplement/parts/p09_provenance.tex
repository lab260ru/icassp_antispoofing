\section{Release}
\label{sec:release}

\textbf{Contents.} Full code, test set, per-item result tables for every
generation scored, both transcript sets (AR and CTC judges'), the
machine-verified formal development of the refuted contraction bound, and
a stratified 165-clip audio sample spanning every model, $k$ band and
outcome, with its selection rule, so any scoring decision can be checked
by ear.

\textbf{Exact revisions.} A checkpoint name does not pin a model;
repositories update in place. Every model (panel synthesisers, vocoder,
non-autoregressive baselines, judge) is pinned to the exact revision the
runs resolved, pins read back from the runs so they cannot drift; the
judge above all: it decides every count, hence every number.

\textbf{Regeneration.} The released pipeline regenerates every number in
both documents from the released artifacts; none is typed by hand. The
formal development ships with a verification gate (builds, no unfinished
proof, no theorem on an unverified axiom); nothing counts as
machine-checked unless the gate passes on the cited release.
