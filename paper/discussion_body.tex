\noindent\textbf{The rival the evidence favours.}
With contraction refuted, the live question is where the count fails: erased
from the state, or surviving there while the policy reading it fails.
Venkatesh~\cite{venkatesh2026repeated} established the representational half of
that in text models by probing; we add the causal half and the isolation of periodicity
from length. The count is not erased: it stays decodable
late ($R^2$ \ProbeLateMed{}), and a probe past the extension horizon is
inconclusive at $n=\PhNCk{}$ (S12). Deciding needs a manipulation of
the readout at fixed state, and we ran two. Splicing whole donor states is too disruptive to
read, an \emph{unrelated} donor moving the count $1.3\times$ as much as one
differing in $k$. A rank-1 patch transferring only the probe's count
coordinate removes the damage (\CausalDegen{}\% degenerate, self-patch
bitwise-identical \CausalNoop{}/\CausalNoop{}) and moves the count not at all
($\CausalShift{}$ log-count $[\CausalShiftLo{},\CausalShiftHi{}]$,
$n=\CausalN{}$); steering along it gives no dose-response. On this checkpoint the count coordinate
transplants cleanly and the decoder ignores it (S26). The patch is a single-checkpoint
result on Llasa-1B, where the Jacobian measurement now spans \JacPanelN{}.

\noindent\textbf{Related work, and what the control decides.}
Closest is representational collapse~\cite{barbero2024glasses}, where distinct
prefixes converge with length --- which our control separates from periodicity.
Counting-specific expressivity results agree by another route --- transformers
count only under conditions on precision, depth and positional
encoding~\cite{yehudai2024when,salzer2025counting,zhang2024counting}, with
single-pass~\cite{hahn2020theoretical,strobl2024formal} and state-tracking
bounds~\cite{li2025statetracking} bracketing it. The two predict differently,
which is the point of the control: an inductive-bias limit is a property of the
model and survives at fixed periodicity, a contraction limit is a property of
the \emph{conditioning}. Ours varies periodicity at fixed length, and the
result is periodicity-specific.

\noindent\textbf{What the design does not separate, and other limits.}
Periodicity and \emph{verbatim token identity} vary together here, so
``periodicity'' is the pair, and neither is separated from \emph{training-set
frequency}: $k$-fold repetition is far rarer in speech corpora than a matched
distinct-word sentence, and an out-of-distribution account predicts the same
dissociation. Inside the repeated arm count and text length are one variable, so
a probe decoding $k$ may be decoding length. The duration story rests on one model per strategy,
the causal patch on one checkpoint, the panel on \NFamilies{} families; a Spanish
arm was unreportable, an exact-rate contrast needing a judge accurate enough to
pin the control side near one (S27).
Exclusions are rule-based and blind to arm and score, and the judge is
validated against concatenative ground truth, not listeners.
