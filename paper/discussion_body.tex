\noindent\textbf{What is proved, and what the measurements do not show.}
The Lean artifact certifies an implication: periodic conditioning plus
contraction implies a finite counting horizon. It does not prove any transformer
contracts, and two attempts to measure $q$ failed --- a boundary-distance
estimator needs a monotone read-head these decoders lack, and a perturbation
probe found no difference in local contraction between repeated items and their
controls ($7$ of $18$ pairs, $p=0.12$), its linearity control failing too (S5).

The theorem's distinctive prediction is a constant $N^\ast$, invisible where our
ladder stopped. Extending to $k=128$ found saturation of the predicted kind but
not one derived from a measured $q$, locating these decoders just below the
collapse regime rather than confirming the bound. The state budget stays positive
while growing at \CapRatio{} of the control rate, and a probe finds the count
degraded, not erased (\ProbeRetRep{} against \ProbeRetCtl{}).

A conditional account risks being unfalsifiable, so we say what would refute it:
a control with its twin's deficit; a model whose capacity gain matches its
control; a probe reading the count as well late as early; or an AR decoder
behaving like the non-AR one.
\ErrBestModel{} has a capacity gap but no deficit and no horizon, so capacity
loss is necessary and not sufficient.

The closest prior result is representational
collapse~\cite{barbero2024glasses}: as sequences grow, distinct prefixes map to
arbitrarily close representations, already implying counting errors. But
collapse-by-length predicts that equally long control and repeated items fail
alike, and they do not wherever the control carries no repetition of its own;
\eqref{eq:horizon} also supplies a rate in repetitions that a
length-indexed bound does not. Dynamical treatments index convergence by depth or an abstract
temperature~\cite{dong2021attention,geshkovski2023emergence,gao2026clustered},
and expressivity bounds by circuit class or state
tracking~\cite{strobl2024formal,merrill2024illusion} constrain one forward pass;
ours indexes generation steps. A competing account holds that in text LMs the count survives in the residual
stream and only the output policy fails~\cite{venkatesh2026repeated}; our probes
favour representational degradation, and deployed
mitigations~\cite{wang2025eliminating,xin2024ralle} act too late to reach it.
