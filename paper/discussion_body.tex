\noindent\textbf{What is proved, and what is not.}
The Lean artifact certifies an implication: periodic conditioning plus
contraction implies a finite counting horizon. It does not prove that any
transformer contracts, and our attempt to measure that premise directly failed
(Section~\ref{sec:negative}). What we establish empirically is the theorem's
\emph{consequence} --- capacity saturation under periodicity --- not its
antecedent. Formalisation is what forced us to keep the two apart.

That deep transformers lose rank and over-squash is
known~\cite{dong2021attention,barbero2024glasses}, and self-attention has been
analysed as a dynamical system with attracting
clusters~\cite{geshkovski2023emergence,gao2026clustered}. Those results index
convergence by \emph{depth} or an abstract temperature; ours indexes it by
\emph{generation step under periodic conditioning}, which turns the consequence
into a statement about counting. A competing account holds that in text LMs the
count survives in the residual stream and only the output policy
fails~\cite{venkatesh2026repeated}; our probes favour representational
degradation, but the two are not exclusive.

\noindent\textbf{Implications and limits.}
Existing mitigations act late~\cite{wang2025eliminating,xin2024ralle}. Under this
account they help only insofar as they raise $q$ or enlarge the readout margin,
and \eqref{eq:horizon} says the levers are unequal: the horizon is logarithmic in
the margin but inversely logarithmic in $q$. XTTS-v2 is a case in point --- with
its shipped repetition penalty disabled, $k^\ast$ falls from \AblRpKStar{} to
\AblNoRpKStar{} while the repeated-vs-control gap persists
(\AblNoRpRep{}\% vs.\ \AblNoRpCtl{}\%), so the penalty was masking the collapse,
not causing it. Limits: the panel is English-only and all six checkpoints are
codec-language models with softmax attention over text, so the claim is about
that architecture class rather than AR TTS in general; the capacity ratio does
not order $k^\ast$ across models, and we do not claim it does; text attention is
unreachable where text and acoustic embeddings are additively fused (Qwen3-TTS);
and we do not propagate the residual $O(\delta + 1/k)$ term of
Lemma~\ref{lem:dilution} through the theorem, so the machine-checked bound applies
to the idealised periodic map rather than the measured one.
