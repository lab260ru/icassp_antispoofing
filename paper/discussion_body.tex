\noindent\textbf{The rival we could not decide.}
With contraction refuted, the live question is whether the count is erased from
the state or survives there while the policy reading it fails. It is not erased:
it stays decodable late ($R^2$ \ProbeLateMed{}; S12), as
Venkatesh~\cite{venkatesh2026repeated} found in text models. Deciding needs a manipulation of the
readout at fixed state, and we could not build one. A rank-1 patch transplanting
the probe's count coordinate moves the count not at all
(\EqPatchQlgFrac{}--\EqPatchLsmFrac{}\% of a full transfer), but on
\ErrBestModel{} neither does a \emph{carrier} direction it decodes perfectly
(\PcCarrierBound{}\%), and the write matches a sampler re-roll on all
\PcRerollNTests{} readouts ($p\ge\PcRerollPMin{}$). It is registered but carries
nothing, steering only at $\PcSteerAlpha{}\times$ its magnitude; on
\PcInertCk{} it is never detectable, bounding only \PcInertBound{}\% at
$n{=}\PcInertN{}$ (S26).

\noindent\textbf{Related work, and what the control decides.}
Closest is representational collapse~\cite{barbero2024glasses}, which our control
separates from periodicity and which we measure: distinguishable states grow at
\CapRatio{} of the control's rate (\CapAdjRatio{} adjusted, disjoint in
\CapSepPhrase{}) but still grow in \CapPosModels{} --- a slowing, not the
theorem's fixed point (S8).
Counting-specific expressivity results agree by another route: transformers
count only under conditions on precision, depth and positional
encoding~\cite{yehudai2024when,salzer2025counting,zhang2024counting}, with
single-pass~\cite{hahn2020theoretical,strobl2024formal} and state-tracking
bounds~\cite{li2025statetracking} bracketing it --- properties of the model,
invariant to period; ours moves with it.

\noindent\textbf{What the design does not separate, and other limits.}
Periodicity is not separated from \emph{training-set frequency}, though the
obvious form of it predicts the wrong arm: under an independent LM the
\emph{control} is the less probable text in \NllIndCtlHigherPct{}\% of pairs
(S8). Text likelihood is not corpus frequency, so it is weakened, not closed.
The duration story rests on one model
per strategy, the panel on \NFamilies{} families, and the Spanish arm needs a
better judge (S27). Exclusions are blind to the count;
the judge is validated on concatenative audio, not listeners.
