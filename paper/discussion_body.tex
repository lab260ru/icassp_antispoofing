\noindent\textbf{What is and is not proved.}
The Lean artifact certifies an implication: periodic conditioning plus
contraction implies a finite counting horizon. It does not prove that any
transformer contracts. That premise is empirical, and where a model fails it the
theorem is silent --- a distinction the formalisation forced us to keep explicit,
and one that descriptive accounts of hallucination tend to blur.

\noindent\textbf{Relation to prior results.}
That deep transformers lose rank and over-squash is
known~\cite{dong2021attention,barbero2024glasses}, and self-attention has been
analysed as a dynamical system with attracting
clusters~\cite{geshkovski2023emergence,gao2026clustered}. Those results index
convergence by \emph{depth} or an abstract temperature; ours indexes it by
\emph{generation step under periodic conditioning}, which turns the consequence
into a statement about counting and yields a horizon in actionable units.
Repetition inevitability has also been derived at the level of decoding
rules~\cite{fu2021theoretical,xu2022learning}; Theorem~\ref{thm:collapse}
constrains any Lipschitz readout and so is decoding-rule agnostic. A competing
account holds that in text LMs the count survives in the residual stream and only
the output policy fails~\cite{venkatesh2026repeated}; our probes favour
representational degradation, but the two are not exclusive and separating them
is the obvious next experiment.

\noindent\textbf{Implications and limits.}
Existing mitigations act late: attention supervision, alignment priors,
contrastive decoding~\cite{wang2025eliminating,xin2024ralle}. Under this account
they help only insofar as they raise $q$ or enlarge the readout margin, and
\eqref{eq:horizon} says the levers are not equal --- the horizon is logarithmic
in the margin but inversely logarithmic in $q$, so moving $q$ from $0.9$ to
$0.95$ roughly doubles it while doubling the margin adds a constant. XTTS-v2 is
itself a case in point: it ships a repetition penalty of $5.0$ on acoustic
tokens, and rerunning it with that penalty disabled moves $k^\ast$ from
\AblRpKStar{} to \AblNoRpKStar{} while the repeated-vs-control gap persists
(\AblNoRpRep{}\% vs.\ \AblNoRpCtl{}\% at $k\ge6$). The penalty was masking the
collapse, not causing it, which makes the penalised model the conservative member
of our panel. Remaining limits: the panel is English-only; text attention is
unavailable where text and acoustic embeddings are additively fused (Qwen3-TTS);
and we do not propagate the residual $O(\delta + 1/k)$ term of
Lemma~\ref{lem:dilution} through the theorem, so the machine-checked bound
applies to the idealised periodic map rather than the measured one.
